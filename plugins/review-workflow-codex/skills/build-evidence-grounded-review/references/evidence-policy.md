# Evidence and writing policy

## Claim records

Create claim records before polished prose. Each record needs a stable claim ID, proposed wording, claim type, importance, maximum defensible strength, and evidence links. Each evidence link needs source ID, full-text or abstract basis, page/section locator, support or contradiction direction, evidence design, independence group, and limitations.

Core claims require full text and a locator. Abstract-only material can support discovery or a tightly qualified preliminary observation, not a central synthesis.

## Claim ceilings

- A single observational study supports “reported” or “observed,” not consensus.
- A trend or consensus statement needs at least two independent sources and qualified language.
- A causal statement needs experimental or mechanistic evidence that actually identifies causation.
- Proxy association does not establish physical equivalence, direct observability, diagnosability, or field readiness.
- Novelty claims must state the searched databases, query concepts, date boundary, and comparison set. Never use an absolute “first” from an unbounded search.

## Semantic citation audit

Audit every consequential cited sentence for source identity, exact locator, support direction, scope, strength, independence, citation distance, and bibliography existence. Flag wrong-source, wrong-locator, reversal, overclaim, abstract-only misuse, orphan reference, missing reference, excessive citation distance, and unsupported novelty separately. Repair the cause at Phase 3, 4, or 5.

## Drafting and newcomer access

Each section contract states its reader question, required claims, necessary definitions, evidence limits, figures/tables, and transition. Explain consensus concepts briefly before using them in synthesis. Prefer concrete terms such as input, measured signal, processing step, evidence, decision, and limitation over unexplained abstract axes or labels.

## Figures and tables

Treat every visual as a claim-bearing object. Record whether it is original, adapted, or reproduced; source/permission; data provenance; caption claim; and accessibility text. A table should expose a comparison the prose cannot show as clearly. A figure should clarify a mechanism, workflow, evidence chain, boundary, or decision—not merely decorate a page.

## Revision policy

Patch stable manuscript blocks using the expected prior hash. Reject stale patches. Preserve unaffected blocks and artifacts. Every reviewer concern is addressed, disagreed with for a recorded reason, or accepted as a limitation. Two broad review rounds are the default ceiling; unresolved P0 findings prevent early stop.
